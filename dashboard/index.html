How the implied value is built

Each protocol earns its multiple from the market itself: the median market-cap / TVL of the snapshot’s live-token protocols. A category uses its own median when at least 3 live tokens trade there; a thinner category borrows the median of its family — categories grouped by what they do (vaults and curators, lending, trading, staking, bridges, RWA, AI, payments) — and a category in no family borrows the global median of all — live tokens. Every multiple on this board rests on at least 3 live comparables, so one wild outlier never sets a price. Then:

implied = TVL × multiple × growth × maturity × chains

  • Growth — 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%, moves value by 80% of the change.
  • Maturity — 0.70 + 0.30 × min(age, 3y) / 3y. Older protocols are more proven; unknown age counts as 1 year.
  • Chains — +3% per extra chain, up to 4 extra. Wider distribution is worth a little more.
  • Clamps — every multiple is kept inside 0.05…1.50 so one outlier cannot run away.

“Records no token” means the snapshot’s record carries no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol — it is not proof the protocol has none. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of a token that already trades are kept off the board. This is not financial advice and not a prediction of a launch.
