    How the implied value is built

    Each protocol is priced off the market itself. First the size curve: the least-squares line through the snapshot’s live-token protocols, log market cap on log TVL — a small protocol’s token trades at a far higher multiple of its TVL than a giant’s, so a flat multiple would overprice the giants and underprice the small ones. Then the category factor: the median residual of the protocol’s own category off that line, shrunk toward 1 when the category is thin — a category uses its own median when at least 3 live tokens trade there; a thinner category borrows the median of its family — categories grouped by what they do (vaults and curators, lending, trading, staking, bridges, RWA, AI, payments) — and a category in no family is priced off the bare curve. Every factor on this board rests on at least 3 live comparables, so one wild outlier never sets a price. Then:

    • Growth — 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%, moves value by 80% of the change.
    • Maturity — 0.70 + 0.30 × min(age, 3y) / 3y. Older protocols are more proven; unknown age counts as 1 year.
    • Chains — +3% per extra chain, up to 4 extra. Wider distribution is worth a little more.
    • Band — the middle half of the live tokens land within the curve’s residual P25…P75, so every row shows that band beside its point value.
